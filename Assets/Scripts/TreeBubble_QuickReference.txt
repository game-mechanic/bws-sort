EDITOR BUTTONS (TreeBubbleManager Inspector):
----------------------------------------------
[Spawn Bubbles In Editor] - Preview bubble layout in Scene view
[Clear Bubbles]           - Remove all spawned bubbles

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

KEY SETTINGS:
-------------

SPAWN DISTRIBUTION:
-------------------
Circular Shape (Only):
  • Uses Poisson disk sampling (uniform random)
  • Radius: How far bubbles spread from center
  • Min Distance: Minimum gap between bubbles (default 0.8)
  • Creates organic, leaf-like pattern

Min Distance Between Bubbles: 0.8
  ↑ Increase = More spacing (fewer overlaps)
  ↓ Decrease = Tighter clustering (may overlap)

Max Spawn Attempts: 30
  ↑ Increase = Better distribution (slower)
  ↓ Decrease = Faster spawn (may cluster)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

TYPICAL WORKFLOW:
-----------------
1. Setup TreeBubbleManager GameObject
2. Assign TreeBubble prefab
3. Create 5 slot GameObjects, assign to array
4. Add CategoryBubbleData entries
5. Click "Spawn Bubbles In Editor" button
6. Adjust settings (shape, radius, count, spacing)
7. Click "Clear Bubbles" → Respawn to test
8. Repeat until satisfied
9. Enter Play mode to test physics & gameplay

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

RECOMMENDED VALUES:
-------------------
For 20 bubbles:
  Radius: 3.0, minDistance: 0.8

For 30 bubbles:
  Radius: 4.0, minDistance: 0.7

For 40 bubbles:
  Radius: 5.0, minDistance: 0.6

For 50 bubbles:
  Radius: 6.0, minDistance: 0.5

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

VISUAL INDICATORS (Scene View):
--------------------------------
Green Circle: Spawn area boundary
Yellow Spheres: Slot positions (where clicked bubbles go)
White Lines: Chains (visible in edit mode too)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

SPAWN DISTRIBUTION:
-------------------

CIRCULAR (Uniform Random):
  ✓ Natural, organic look
  ✓ No visible pattern
  ✓ Even distribution throughout
  ✓ Perfect for "leaves on tree" aesthetic
  ✓ Professional game feel

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

TROUBLESHOOTING DISTRIBUTION:
------------------------------

Problem: Bubbles too clustered
Solution: 
  • Increase minDistanceBetweenBubbles
  • Increase radius
  • Decrease bubbleCount

Problem: Bubbles too sparse
Solution:
  • Decrease minDistanceBetweenBubbles
  • Decrease radius
  • Increase bubbleCount

Problem: Empty spots in spawn area
Solution:
  • Increase maxSpawnAttempts
  • Decrease minDistanceBetweenBubbles
  • Increase bubbleCount
